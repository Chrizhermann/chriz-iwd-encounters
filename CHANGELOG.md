# Changelog

## Unreleased

- 300: Belhifet's final fight. Immune to spell levels 1–3 instead of all levels,
  immune to fire again as in the base game (SCS lowers it to 50 %), protected from
  hold and paralysis (as the game's golems are) and from what would end the fight
  without taking his hit points, +2 weapons hit him, more hit
  points from Core Rules up (base game 350: 500, 625, 750; final values, SCS's
  staying power does not add to them), Improved Invisibility
  when he appears and Haste for 30 seconds out of every 42 from Core Rules up, his
  cornugons as a second wave below two
  thirds of his hit points (a third on Insane below one third) and fire immune
  again, no dispel at
  Poquelin's transformation, SCS 35.21's teleport pause repaired (as shipped he
  teleports every 6 seconds on average). The ending plays as in the base game.
  Works with and without SCS.
- 310: Yxunomei and her temple. Her three yuan-ti mages come when the fight with her
  starts (not when her archers first see the party) and walk to the party; as in the
  base game they stay only on Hard and Insane. Her two champions, if they still
  live, leave the torture chamber for her side when she appears. One round later
  two yuan-ti elites come into her room on Hard, three on Insane. The pentagram's
  traps in front of her go off only for the party. SCS 35.21's balor telekinesis,
  which IWD:EE lacks, no longer costs her a round of spells. Works with and without
  SCS.
- 320: the orog raid on Kuldahar. Once one raider or defender wakes, every Avenger
  wakes and the orogs come for the party. The three Marauders, the leaders, charge
  three rounds later after a war horn, with 70 hit points, two attacks per round and
  THAC0 10; the Avengers have 45 hit points. The orogs fight back against the town's
  defenders, raiders who never woke leave after four days like the others, and the
  town alarm no longer brings out a second, living Arundel. Works with and without
  SCS.
- 330: Joril's throne room. Joril has 185 hit points; he, his bodyguards and his guards
  use the Berserker rage of SCS's kit, the bodyguards cast their memorised Hold
  Person. When Gorg turns hostile, his eight winter wolves turn with him and come to
  him. The peaceful handover of Joril's badge no longer pays its experience twice.
  Works with and without SCS.
- 340: Maiden Ilmadia's forge. Ilmadia has 135 hit points and uses the abilities of
  the level-12 Blackguard SCS makes her (Aura of Despair, Poison Weapon three times,
  Absorb Health). Once she turns hostile, her fire giants and drow turn with her and
  the giants gather at her side; the drow bless the giants (component 200, or this
  one without it). Meeting the elementals and salamanders of an SCS team no longer
  turns the forge hostile before her dialogue, and SCS's giants check her death under
  her real name. Works with and without SCS.
- 350: the Idol. One spell per round instead of a random lethal spell every three
  seconds, each on its own cooldown and only at targets it can affect, no Power Word:
  Kill; Flame Strike only where its caster-level ability works. 225 hit points, it
  stays on its plinth. When it is attacked, the greater mummies and zombie lords
  around it come to it. The zombie lords' aura runs again, and each badge door counts
  once, so the counter can no longer lock the way to Poquelin. Works with and without
  SCS.
- 360: Poquelin in the Ascension Cave. 420 hit points (he escapes at half, so his
  waves now matter); below three quarters he jumps away, calls Fire Storm around
  himself, and the next wave comes at once. Nothing kills or removes him outright any
  more (that skipped his escape and the move to Easthaven), and his escape cutscenes
  find him by his placement's name. The finale's Poquelin is unchanged. Works with and
  without SCS.
- 370: Cryshal-Tirith. Pomab's five images carry his name. Each time the real Pomab is
  hit, he and every image left jump to other points and the images hide again what a
  revealing spell showed; between shots they keep their distance. His Cryshal Sentries
  go for whoever last attacked him and for enemies close to him, have 240 hit points and
  play dead for 10 seconds instead of 20 from Core Rules up. Every hit on him now removes
  an image (his own full heal could come first), his script reads the image counter, a
  sentry healed while it plays dead still stands up, and the sentries' caster targeting
  works. Works with and without SCS.
- 380: Mebdinga's barrow. Her wails come on time (her floating lines no longer push her
  cast timer back, and stop once she is hostile), and she runs from an attacker at most
  once every three rounds. When her team turns hostile, the barrow wight and drowned
  dead nearest to her join her team and come to her (two of each on Hard and Insane);
  she sends her War Cry to one of them, and the wailing virgins send theirs only to male
  allies that are not hasted yet. On Hard and Insane, once below half her hit points,
  she shows it and lets out an Undying Lament a round later. Hostile before the talk,
  she no longer stays immortal and the party learns of the soul. The barrow's spawn file
  runs again the ten difficulty gates SCS replaced with its team scripts. Works with and
  without SCS.
- 390: Icasaracht's lair. Icasaracht has 650 hit points (400), her soul gem 250 (150).
  The fallback for an Icasaracht who dies without her last-hit-point cutscene no longer
  creates a second gem and fills the gem's ring slot as the normal path does. SCS's
  second underpriestess gets the random items of the first; both lose their five
  attacks per round (a typo). Works with and without SCS.
- 400: Lord Maluradek. A party member he banishes to room 1 or 2 now wakes that room's
  four animated plates (only the plates themselves raised the alarm, and they appeared
  only once it was raised); the plates fight. 350 hit points (250). Below half his hit
  points all three rooms wake and he fights in melee between his spells. The first
  beholder holds still during the dance again, and he wears his immunity gloves (they sat in a
  quick slot, where they did nothing). Works with and without SCS.
- 410: the Luremaster. He opens the fight with his Improved Invisibility and, while he has
  spells, hops away by Dimension Door when an enemy reaches him (SCS had taken his hop
  script off). His Dispel Magic entry checks the spell he memorises, so his three Dispel
  Magic come. A fallen spectral hero brings its replacement again (up to six). Works with
  and without SCS.
